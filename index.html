Count every push.

The run and walk tracker for moms with a stroller. MomPace counts the calories you burn pushing your baby: stroller weight, baby weight, hills and all.

Your stroller counts

Pushing a stroller takes real effort. MomPace adds it in, based on your stroller, your baby's weight, the wheels and the ground you're on.

Hills count too

Every climb is measured and added, so a hilly walk gets the credit it deserves.

Works without GPS

Lost signal under the trees? MomPace switches to your phone's step counter and back again on its own.

Carrier walks

Wearing your baby instead? Carrier walks count the extra weight you're carrying.
