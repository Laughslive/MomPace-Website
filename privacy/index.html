Privacy policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

The short version: MomPace keeps your workouts on your phone. There's no account and no sign-up. We don't have a server that stores your workouts, and we never sell your data.

This policy explains what the MomPace app ("MomPace", "we", "us") uses on your phone, why, and where it goes. MomPace is run by [YOUR NAME OR BUSINESS NAME].

What the app uses

  • Location (GPS). While a workout is running, MomPace uses your location to measure distance, speed and hills, and to draw your route. If you allow it, tracking keeps going while your screen is off or you're in another app, so a workout isn't cut short. MomPace doesn't use your location when no workout is running.
  • Motion and fitness. MomPace uses your phone's step counter to keep measuring when GPS is weak or when you choose step-counter mode, and it may use the phone's air-pressure sensor to estimate hills.
  • What you enter. Your body weight, your stroller's weight and details, your baby's weight, your carrier's weight and your unit settings. These are used only to calculate calories.
  • Your workout history. Date, time, distance, time moving, calories, hills climbed, step count and the route of each workout.

Where your information is kept

All of the above is stored only on your phone. We don't receive it, and we can't see it. If you delete a workout in the app, it's gone. If you delete the app, its data is deleted with it.

Your phone's own backup service (such as iCloud or Google backup) may include app data in your backups. Those backups are handled by Apple or Google under their privacy policies.

Maps

To show your route on a map, the app uses Apple Maps on iPhone and Google Maps on Android. To draw the map, your phone asks Apple or Google for map images of that area. Those requests are covered by Apple's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy. We don't send your route or workout to anyone.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment card or billing details.

We use a service called RevenueCat to check whether you have an active subscription or free trial. RevenueCat receives a random app ID (not your name or email) and your purchase details, such as which plan you bought and when it renews. It doesn't receive your workouts, location or weights.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your information.
  • We don't show ads, and we don't share your information with advertisers.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking tools in the app. If that ever changes, we'll update this policy first.

Children

MomPace is made for adults. Your baby's weight is something you enter yourself, it's used only to calculate calories, and it stays on your phone. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Your choices

  • You can turn off location or motion access at any time in your phone's settings. Some features won't work without them.
  • You can delete any workout in the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data.
  • Because we don't hold your workout data, there's nothing on our side to request, export or delete. For purchase records, contact us and we'll help.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here and change the date at the top. If a change affects how your information is used, we'll also mention it in the app.
